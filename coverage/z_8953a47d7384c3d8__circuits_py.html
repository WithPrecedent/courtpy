35 """Adds the ideology of the active judges of each case's circuit. 

36 

37 For a case of a court of appeals, the circuit is the court's own; for a 

38 case of a district court, it is the circuit of the court of appeals that 

39 hears its appeals (see `courtpy.courts.circuits`). The judges of the 

40 circuit are those of its court of appeals in a roster (see `Roster`) who 

41 were active on the day the case was decided: they had been appointed, 

42 had not left the court, and had not taken senior status (senior judges 

43 do not sit on the court en banc). Their scores are summarized: 

44 

45 | Column | Meaning | 

46 | --- | --- | 

47 | `circuit_median_party` | The median party of the presidents who appointed them (-1 for Democratic and 1 for Republican). | 

48 | `circuit_total_party` | The sum of those parties, which is how many more were appointed by Republicans than by Democrats. | 

49 | `circuit_median_jcs` | The median of their Judicial Common Space scores (higher is more conservative). | 

50 | `circuit_total_jcs` | The sum of their scores. | 

51 | `circuit_judges` | How many judges were active. | 

52 

53 The scores of the Judicial Common Space are added if the roster has them 

54 (make it with `Roster.from_fjc(jcs = True)`). A judge without a score is 

55 left out of its summaries. A case whose circuit or date is not known (or 

56 whose circuit had no active judge in the roster) gets missing values. 

57 

58 Like every merger of `amos`, it never adds, removes, or reorders rows, 

59 and records how many rows it matched in the dataset's history. It takes 

60 these parameters: 

61 

62 | Parameter | Meaning | 

63 | --- | --- | 

64 | `source` | The roster: the path of a saved one, a roster, or the table of one. Without it, the roster saved in "judges" in `utilities.data_folder()` is used. | 

65 | `columns` | The scores to summarize: "party", "jcs", or both (the default, if the roster has "jcs"). | 

66 | `date` | The column with the date each case was decided. It is "date_filed" unless another is set. | 

67 | `court_num`, `court` | The columns with courtpy's number for each case's court and CourtListener's id for it. They are "court_num" and "court" unless others are set. | 

68 | `prefix` | Text to put before the names of the added columns. It is "circuit_" unless another is set. |
