38 """Estimates the effect of a judge's colleagues on the judge's vote. 

39 

40 The data is `Votes` (see `cases_to_votes`): a row for each judge on 

41 each case, with the judge's vote (such as "vote_reversal"), the party of 

42 the president who appointed the judge ("judge_party", -1 or 1), and the 

43 mean party of the judge's colleagues on the panel ("colleagues_party"). 

44 The vote is regressed on the colleagues' party, the judge's own party, 

45 and (with "interact") their product, which says whether a judge of one 

46 party is moved more by the other party's colleagues, with a fixed effect 

47 for each court in each year, so that each vote is compared only with 

48 the votes of its own court and year (a linear probability model for a 

49 vote of yes or no). 

50 

51 Panels are assigned at random within a court (or so the design 

52 assumes; see `audit_random_assignment`), so the effects are causal if 

53 that holds. Two tests say how sure the estimates are: 

54 

55 * The standard errors are clustered by case, since the judges of a case 

56 vote together, with Stata's correction for few clusters, and give the 

57 p-values and 95% confidence intervals. 

58 * A randomization test reassigns each vote's colleagues' party at random 

59 among the votes of the same court and year `n_permutations` times, and 

60 its p-value is the share of reassignments whose estimate is at least 

61 as far from 0 as the one found. It assumes nothing about the 

62 distribution of the votes. 

63 

64 The table has a row for each term, with "coefficient", "std_error", 

65 "t_statistic", "p_value", "ri_p_value" (for the colleagues' party and 

66 its product with the judge's party), "ci_lower", and "ci_upper". 

67 

68 """

204def _clustered_errors( 

205 x: np.ndarray, 

206 residuals: np.ndarray, 

207 groups: np.ndarray, 

208 absorbed: int) -> np.ndarray: 

209 """Returns standard errors clustered by group. 

210 

211 Args: 

212 x: the regression's columns, with the strata's means removed. 

213 residuals: the regression's errors. 

214 groups: the cluster of each row, from 0. 

215 absorbed: the number of fixed effects that removing the strata's 

216 means absorbed, which count against the degrees of freedom. 

217 

218 Returns: 

219 The standard error of each coefficient, with Stata's correction for 

220 few clusters: G / (G - 1) times (N - 1) / (N - K), where G is 

221 the number of clusters, N of rows, and K of coefficients and 

222 fixed effects. 

223 

224 """ 

225 rows, width = x.shape 

226 clusters = int(groups.max()) + 1 

227 scores = np.column_stack([ 

228 np.bincount(groups, weights = x[:, c] * residuals, 

229 minlength = clusters) 

230 for c in range(width)]) 

231 bread = np.linalg.pinv(x.T @ x) 

232 covariance = bread @ (scores.T @ scores) @ bread 

233 freedom = max(rows - width - absorbed, 1) 

234 correction = ( 

235 clusters / max(clusters - 1, 1) * (rows - 1) / freedom) 

236 errors: np.ndarray = np.sqrt( 

237 np.clip(np.diag(covariance) * correction, 0, None)) 

238 return errors
