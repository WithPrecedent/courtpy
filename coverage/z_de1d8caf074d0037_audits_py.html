76 """Checks that the traits of cases are balanced across their panels. 

77 

78 Studies of panels (such as of whether a judge votes differently beside 

79 judges appointed by the other party) rely on panels being assigned to 

80 cases at random within a court. If they are, a trait of a case that the 

81 panel cannot change (such as whether it is criminal) has nothing to do 

82 with the panel's composition within each court in each year. 

83 

84 For each trait, the table has its mean for each composition of the panel 

85 ("DDD" to "RRR"; see `courtpy.judges.panel_compositions`), the 

86 correlation between the trait and the number of Republican appointees 

87 within each court and year (each stratum's mean removed), and the share 

88 of `n_permutations` random reassignments of the compositions within each 

89 court and year whose correlation is at least as far from 0 (a 

90 permutation test, with no assumption about how the trait is 

91 distributed). A trait is balanced if that share is at least `alpha`. 

92
